Who we are

Noventra backs founders building what comes next. We are stage-agnostic, investing in companies working at the edge of advanced technology and in the sectors that shape industrial and national capability for decades ahead. We look for teams who have already done the difficult part — built something real, gathered people around it, and put it in front of the world. Capital alone rarely decides an outcome. What decides it is who stands behind the company when the work gets hard. If you are building something ambitious and intend to see it through, we would like to hear from you.
